Questions parents ask

Do you collect my child from home?

Yes. We collect your child at home, wait for 2 minutes, then drop them at school. In the afternoon we collect from school and take them home again, at times we agree together.

Is your driver properly licensed?

Yes. Our driver holds a PrDP, first endorsed in 2013, with 13 years of professional driving in the Western Cape and 4 years of safely transporting learners.

Which schools do you cover?

Wolraad Woltemade PS, Bosmansdam PS, Bothasig HS, Bosmansdam HS, Edgemead PS and Edgemead HS, plus daycares in Bothasig and Edgemead.

What about extramurals?

No problem. Extramural transport is an extra R300 per month. Tell us the days and times when you message us.

How do I book a seat?

Send us a WhatsApp message with your child’s school and your suburb. We reply with availability and confirm your pick-up times.
